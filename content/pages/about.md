I'm just a normal everyday person who tries to get on with life, earn a living and live simply and happily. The trouble is that a lot of what gets imposed on us is dressed up as something we should be grateful for, when it plainly isn't. Fake governments have made ordinary life harder than it needs to be, and the people who benefit from that arrangement rarely get asked about it.

I've written this website as simple lists so you can easily click on something, see what the actual claim is, what the alternatives are, who is responsible, what we need them to do, and whether they have done it yet.

My aim is to expose the companies and organisations feeding us this stuff, and then give them the opportunity to put it right and get off the lists. Some of them will. Hopefully good companies see that and create rivals worth supporting instead. There are some good companies out there.

I've tried to write this site in simple, straight-to-the-point language. I think it needs it. I'm not being crude — I'm being clear. The people who need to know exactly what we think should know exactly what we think.

## How this site is built

No tracking scripts, no advertising networks, no third-party cookies, and no database behind it. The pages are plain files that load quickly and work on any device. The only thing collected is what you choose to send through the contact form, and you can read exactly what happens to it on the <a href="page:privacy">Privacy Policy</a> page.
