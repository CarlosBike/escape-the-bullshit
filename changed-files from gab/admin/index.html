First time here

  1. You need to be on the published Netlify address for this to sign in — not localhost. On localhost it will not sign in, and that is expected rather than a fault.
  2. Sign in with the email address you registered with Netlify. You will get a confirmation link — click it.
  3. Choose Pages or Topics from the list on the left.
  4. Pick a topic, change a sentence, and press Save.
  5. Netlify rebuilds the site by itself. Reload the site in your browser to see the change.

If sign-in does not work

On Netlify, check these three things:

  1. Identity → Enable Identity is switched on.
  2. Identity → Registration → Confirm email is set, so that only you can register.
  3. Identity → Services → Git Gateway → Enable is switched on, and you have allowed access to your one repository.

Also check that site_url and display_url in admin/config.yml match your real Netlify address.

Adding something new

The editing screen can change anything that is already there. To add a brand new page or topic, copy a block in admin/config.yml and change its file and label — or just ask me to do it.

About the wording

Several pages cover health, dental and financial matters. They say plainly when a question needs a doctor, a dentist or a qualified adviser rather than a website. Please keep those notes in place when you rewrite a page — they matter for the reader and for the site's credibility.
