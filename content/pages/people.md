This is the most important page. Here we can show our strength and willingness to fight back against the tyranny that is going to be defeated by us.

We need to know how many people are willing to take action. It is not a big organisation we need to build. It is a lot of people doing one specific thing each, in small groups, in their own area and their own industry. That has always worked better than waiting for one big group to form — and the internet makes it possible now in a way it never was before.
