Last updated: 19 November 2026. This policy applies to the Escape the Bullshit website.

The short version: this site does not run advertising, does not use tracking cookies, and does not have a database of readers. The only personal data it holds is what you deliberately send through the contact form, and how much of it you choose to give.

## Who is responsible for this site

Escape the Bullshit is an independent site operated by its author. The data controller is the site owner. The contact email for data questions is [[email]].

The site is hosted by a third-party hosting provider. The provider processes technical data on the site's behalf under the site owner's instructions, in order to serve the pages and keep the service running.

## What data is collected

### If you use the contact form

The form collects your name, email address, an optional subject and your message. The consent checkbox on the form sets the lawful basis for processing this information: your explicit consent.

### If you write a comment

If commenting is enabled on a page, your name, email address, the content of your comment, and the time you posted it are recorded. Your email address is not published.

### Technical data collected automatically

The hosting provider records standard server logs — IP address, browser type, requested page, and time — for security and troubleshooting. These logs are retained for a short period and are not used to build a profile of you.

### What is not collected

There are no advertising or analytics trackers, no third-party marketing cookies, no social-media widgets that track visitors, and no cross-site profiling of readers. If this changes, this page will be updated before it does.

## Cookies

The site itself sets no cookies. If comments are enabled, a small cookie is used by the commenting system to help manage the form and prevent spam. No advertising or profiling cookies are used.

## Why the data is used, and on what basis

Contact and comment data is used to reply to you, to publish a comment if you asked for it to be published, and to keep the site secure. Server logs are used for security and to diagnose faults. Nothing is sold, rented or shared for marketing.

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, the lawful bases relied on are your consent (for contact and comment data) and legitimate interests (for basic security and operating logs).

## Who else sees it

Your information is shared only with the hosting provider, and with a regulator or law enforcement body if we are legally required to. We do not sell data, and we do not pass it to advertisers or data brokers.

## Where it is stored

Data is stored on servers used by the hosting provider. Some of those servers may be located outside the UK. Where data is transferred outside the UK, the transfer is covered by the UK's approved transfer mechanism — either UK adequacy regulations for the destination, or standard contractual clauses with a transfer risk assessment.

## How long it is kept

- **Contact messages:** kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and follow it up, and then deleted. As a working rule, up to 12 months.
- **Comments:** published comments remain visible for as long as the page exists. Comments containing personal data that you ask to be removed will be removed.
- **Server logs:** typically retained for no more than 30 days, then deleted automatically.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- be told what personal data is held about you, and get a copy of it;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- have data erased, where there is no overriding reason to keep it;
- restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests;
- receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format;
- withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

To use any of these, email [[email]]. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at **ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint**.

## Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to the hosting account and to any mailbox is protected by strong, unique passwords and, where available, multi-factor authentication. No system is perfectly secure, but nothing beyond what is described here is collected.

## Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and personal data from children under 13 is not knowingly collected. If you believe a child has sent personal information through the contact form, email the address above and it will be deleted.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be mentioned on the site's main page.
